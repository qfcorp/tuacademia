This manual explains, step by step and with pictures, how to run a league or championship in TuAcademia (football, indoor football, ecuavóley, volleyball, basketball and other team sports, and also individual sports: car and bicycle racing, tennis, padel, table tennis, martial arts, boxing, bowling, swimming and athletics), from your first login to the close of each matchday. You do not need any computer knowledge: just follow the red numbers in each picture.

📅 Not using TuAcademia yet? Request your demo (7 days with all modules) at tuacademia.tukanec.com/solicitar-demo or write to us on WhatsApp. We will set up your access and help you load your league or school.

1. How to use this manual and who uses what

TuAcademia is an online system: there is nothing to install. It opens in the browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari) on your computer or phone, at your league's address, for example tuacademia.tukanec.com.

How to read the pictures: each screenshot has red circles with numbers (1, 2, 3…) and a red border around the important part. Below the picture you will find what each number is and what you should do there.

Words that appear often in this manual:

  • Tap or click: press with your finger (phone) or with the left mouse button (computer).

  • Menu: the list of options on the left (on a phone, open it with the ☰ button at the top).

  • Save: the green button at the end of each form. If you do not tap it, your changes are lost.

  • Championship or tournament: a competition (for example, "Clausura 2026"). A league can have several at the same time.

  • Matchday or round: a round of matches (Matchday 1, Matchday 2…).

  • Scorekeeping: the official match sheet: players present, goals, cards and signatures.

2. The complete path of a championship

The first time, follow these 8 steps in order. When a new season starts, just repeat from step 3: the settings, teams and players stay saved.

  1. Settings (section 5): enter the league's name, logo, colors, fields and bank account.

  2. Teams and players (section 6): create the teams and load the players with a photo, or let each delegate register them from their phone (section 17).

  3. Championship (section 7): create the championship and set its rules (how many players, periods, points, cards and amounts to charge).

  4. Draw and fixtures (section 8): select the teams that play and tap Hold draw. The full calendar is built automatically.

  5. Schedule (section 9): assign the day, time, field and referees to each match, automatically or manually.

  6. Match day (sections 10 and 11): the scorekeeper fills in the sheet from their phone and the referee sends their report.

  7. Everything is calculated automatically (section 12): standings, top scorers, suspensions, bulletin and social media images.

  8. Charges and receipts (section 14): registrations, referee fees and fines, with receipts and card payments.

3. Logging in and My account

To log in you need the email and password that QF Producciones (or your league administrator) gave you. It is the same screen for administrators, scorekeepers, referees and delegates: the system recognizes who you are and shows you only your part.

  1. Email: type your full email address, for example admin@ligaelejido.ec.

  2. Password: type your password. Upper and lower case matter.

  3. Log in: tap the green button. You will enter the Overview screen (section 4).

  4. Forgot your password?: if you do not remember it, tap this link, type your email and you will receive a message to create a new one.

Tip: on your phone, open the system's address and choose "Add to home screen" or "Install". An icon will appear like any other app.

My account and security

In the top menu, tap My account. This is where you protect your access.

  • Change my password: type your current password, then the new one twice (at least 8 characters) and tap Change password.

  • Two-step verification (recommended for administrators): tap Enable two-step verification. Install an authenticator app on your phone (Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator), scan the code that appears and type the 6 digits. From then on, in addition to your password, you will be asked for those 6 digits when you log in; they change every 30 seconds. This way no one can get in even if they know your password.

  • Log out: when you use someone else's computer, always tap Log out when you finish.

4. The Overview screen

This is the first screen after you log in. It shows at a glance how the league is doing and tells you what is still missing in the setup. To come back here from anywhere, tap Overview in the menu.

  1. Main menu: from here you reach every part of the system. Each option has its own section in this manual. The red number (for example on Requests) tells you there is something new to review.

  2. League bar: shows your league's name, the format (for example 7-a-side football) and the View public page link, which opens the page the public sees. It also shows your profile (Administrator).

  3. Figures: how many teams, active players and championships you have, and the format of the current championship.

  4. Getting started: a list of what is missing to get the league ready. What is already done appears with ✅ and crossed out; what is missing appears with ⬜. Tap any line and it takes you straight to that screen. When you complete all 8 steps, this box disappears.

  5. Manage championship: opens the current championship (section 7).

  6. Upcoming matches: the scheduled matches, with day, time and field.

  7. Latest results: the scores of the matches already played.

5. League settings

This is where you enter the league's fixed details: name, logo, colors, fields and bank account. You fill them in only once at the start. Go to the Settings menu.

  1. Fields: the list of fields where matches are played. To add one, type the Name (for example "Field 1") and, if you like, the Address; then tap + Add field. If a field cannot be used for a while, tap Deactivate (it is not deleted, it simply stops being used in the schedule). Delete removes it permanently.

  2. League name: the official name shown on the public page, ID cards, scorekeeping sheets and receipts.

  3. League logo: tap Choose File (the browser's "Choose File" button) and select the logo image from your computer or phone.

  4. Primary and secondary color: tap the color box and choose. The primary color is used for buttons, bars and headings on the public page.

  5. Payment details: type the bank, account type and number, the account holder and the RUC (Ecuador's taxpayer number). Delegates see these details when they pay by bank transfer.

Further down on the same screen there are other options:

  • Main sponsor and its logo: they appear on the social media images.

  • League contact email: it appears in the privacy policy.

  • Transfers between teams: the Transfer fee ($) charged when a player changes team.

  • Age category (only if your league plays by categories): name (for example Under-15 or Masters 40+), minimum and maximum age, the date on which age is counted and how many "over-age" players are allowed.

  • Telegram notifications (optional): check Use Telegram notifications, choose what to notify (results, live goals, schedule, notices to delegates and referees), tap Link a channel or group and follow the on-screen instructions. Test it with Send test message.

At the end, tap the green Save button.

Important: the rules you set here are the default rules. Each new championship copies them when it is created, and they can then be changed for that championship only (section 7). Existing championships do not change.

6. Teams and players

Teams and their players are created only once and are used for all championships. There are three ways to load players: one by one, from an Excel file, or by letting each delegate register them from their phone (section 17). Go to the Teams menu.

Team list

  1. + New team: takes you to the form below (number 4).

  2. Import from Excel: to load many teams and players at once (see below).

  3. Team card: tap any team to open it. It shows how many players it has and the maximum allowed (for example 12 / 18). The complete label means it already has the minimum number of players to play.

  4. New team: type the Name (required), a short Abbreviation (for example LDU), upload the Crest, choose the Color, and enter the Delegate's name and phone number. Tap Create team.

Inside a team

  1. Print team ID cards: generates the ID cards of all players, ready to print (section 15).

  2. Account statement: what the team owes and what it has paid (section 14). History shows all the changes made to the team.

  3. Edit team details: to change the name, crest, color or delegate.

  4. Players: each card is a player with their number, photo, name and position. Tap a card to open and edit the player.

  5. Add player: fill in First names (required), Last names, ID card number, Shirt number, Position and Date of birth. In Player's photo tap Choose File; on a phone you can take the photo right then. Check the Data protection boxes (the player authorized the use of their data and, if they wish, the publication of their photo). Tap Add player.

  6. Delegate access: this is where the team delegate's user is created. Type their name, email and a password (at least 8 characters) and save it. Give them those details: they use them to log in from their phone. The Change password, Deactivate and Delete buttons are used to manage that access.

Edit a player, deregister them or transfer them

  • At the top are the Print ID card, View QR verification and Change history buttons, along with their goals and cards in all championships.

  • In Edit player correct whatever you need and tap Save. To change the photo use New photo.

  • Deregister: uncheck Active player and save. The player no longer appears, but their statistics are kept.

  • Transfer to another team: choose the Receiving team and the Transfer fee. That amount is charged to the receiving team's account (0 = no cost).

Import from Excel

  1. Step 1: tap Download Excel template. Open the file, delete the two example players and type one player per row. In the Team column, repeat the team name for each player; if the team does not exist, the system creates it.

    • Required: Team and Player's first names.

    • Date of birth in day/month/year format (example 15/03/1995). If the player is under 18, fill in the parent or guardian's details.

    • Photos do not go in the Excel file: they are uploaded later in each team or by each delegate.

  2. Step 2: in File tap Choose File and select the completed Excel file. If you like, choose the championship in which the teams are registered.

  3. Tap Review the file. Nothing is saved yet: you will see the list so you can check for errors (for example a mistyped ID card number). If everything is fine, confirm to save.

7. Championships and their rules

A championship is each competition (Apertura, Clausura, Cup…). Each one has its own rules: how many players, how long a match lasts, how many points a win gives, when a player is suspended and how much is charged. Go to the Championships menu.

  1. Championship card: shows the name, the status (In play or Finished), the format, the duration and how many matches have been played. Tap it to manage it (draw, fixtures, matches, standings).

  2. Copy a championship for the new season: the fastest way to start a season. Tap it, choose the previous championship in Copy from, type the Name of the new one and the Season, and tap Create copy. The same rules are copied and, if you wish, the same teams.

  3. Name: type the name of the new championship, for example "Apertura 2027". The Season is filled in automatically with the year.

  4. Format: choose Round-robin (a single table) or By groups (A, B, C…). In Laps (legs) choose Single leg or Home and away.

Leave the Register all the league's teams box checked if all of them play. If it is an age-based championship (Under-12, Masters…), check Age category.

8. Draw and fixtures

The draw assigns the teams at random and automatically builds the calendar of every matchday (the fixtures). There is no need to build it by hand. Go to Championships, tap the championship and you will land on the 1. Teams and draw tab.

  1. Championship tabs: the work goes in order: 1. Teams and draw, 2. Fixtures and results, 3. Standings and statistics, 4. Cards and sanctions. In Settings you change the rules. Above the tabs are the championship's public links (standings, fixtures, live) with a copy button to send them via WhatsApp.

  2. Registered teams: check the teams that play in this championship and uncheck those that do not. Tap Save registration.

  3. Hold draw: before tapping it, leave Generate the fixtures after the draw checked and, if you like, fill in the Date of matchday 1, the Days between matchdays (7 = every week), the Time and the Default field. Then tap Hold draw.

  4. View animated draw: opens the draw as a full-screen animation, ideal for projecting at the delegates' meeting or in a broadcast.

The fixtures: matchdays and matches

After the draw, open the 2. Fixtures and results tab. There you will see all the matchdays with their matches.

  1. Schedule automatically: the system assigns days, times, fields and referees for an entire matchday (section 9).

  2. Schedule manually: to set the time and field of each match yourself (section 9).

  3. Automatic knockout brackets: when the regular phase ends, it builds the round of 16, quarterfinals, semifinal and final with the top teams in the standings. Add match manually is used to create a single extra match (for example, a tiebreaker).

  4. Matchday scorekeeping sheets: prints the sheets for all the matches of that matchday at once, to take them to the field.

  5. Match: each card shows the teams, the score (if already played), the day and the field. Tap it to open the match (section 10).

If you got the teams wrong: use Regenerate regular-season fixtures. This deletes the previous calendar, so only use it before any matches are played.

Full fixtures for printing and championship outline (Professional plan)

With the Professional plan, the 🗺 Full fixtures and championship outline box appears at the top of the 2. Fixtures and results tab. Use it to hand the full calendar to the delegates and so that everyone can see, from day one, how the championship will run all the way to the final.

  1. Print full fixtures: opens a document with all the matchdays (date, time, field, home team, result, away team and who has a bye) and the final rounds. Tap Print / Save PDF.

  2. Blank fixtures (to fill in by hand): the same document, but with ____ - ____ instead of the results, to write them down at the field.

  3. Print only the outline: a landscape sheet with the groups and the final-phase bracket.

  4. View the championship outline: the championship's path (for example Regular phase (5 matchdays) → Semifinal → Final), a box per group with its teams, matchdays and matches, and the bracket with slots such as 1st Group A vs 2nd Group B and Winner Semifinal 1 vs Winner Semifinal 2, up to the Champion box.

  5. Planned final phase: choose how many teams qualify per group (or from the standings) and whether a third-place match is played, then tap Save planned final phase. If you choose No final phase, the team at the top of the standings wins.

When the regular phase ends and you create the Automatic knockout brackets, the outline switches to showing the real matches: each slot is filled with the qualified team and, once played, with the score and penalties. The cross-group pairings are planned: the final bracket is ordered by points at the end of the regular phase.

On the public page, the Fixtures tab also shows the 🖨 Print full fixtures and 🗺 Championship outline buttons, so that delegates and the public can download them on their own.

The printable fixtures and the outline are exclusive to the Professional plan. In the Basic and Tournament plans, and in the demo, the box appears with a padlock 🔒 and a button to see the Professional plan.

9. Scheduling: day, time, field and referees

Scheduling means deciding when and where each match is played and who referees it. The system can do it for you: it distributes the matches so that no team or referee has two matches at the same time, alternates early and late slots, and shares the work among the referees. Open the championship, go to the 2. Fixtures and results tab and tap Schedule automatically.

The screen has 3 numbered steps:

  1. What are you going to schedule? Check the matchday or matchdays you want to schedule (for example Matchday 5). Next to it you can see how many matches it has and how many have already been played. Check Also reschedule matches that already have a date and time only if you want to change what was already scheduled.

  2. When and where?

    • Date: the day of the first matchday chosen.

    • Days between matchdays: if you chose several matchdays, 7 = one per week.

    • First match at and Last match at: the time window in which matches can be played that day.

    • Minutes per turn: how long each field slot lasts (match + break + margin). The system suggests a value.

    • Fields: check the available fields. If today you are using a field that is not on the list, type it in Another field.

  3. Referees: leave Assign referees automatically checked and uncheck the referees who are not available that day. The option Prevent a referee from officiating the same team two matchdays in a row helps avoid complaints.

Tap View proposal. The Proposal (not saved yet) appears with each match, time, field and referees. If you like it, tap ✓ Save this schedule. If not, change the data and tap View proposal again.

Schedule manually

On the same screen, tap the Manual tab. Choose the Matchday. The list of matches appears, and for each one you can type the Date, Time, Field and choose the Referees. At the end tap Save schedule. It is also useful for correcting a single match after using automatic scheduling.

Tip: after saving, share the schedule with the social media image (section 16) or turn on Telegram notifications (section 5), and the delegates and referees will find out automatically.

10. The match from the computer

Each match has its own screen. From here you enter the result (if the scorekeeping on the phone was not used), send the link to the scorekeeper, assign the referees and print the scorekeeping sheet. Open the championship, go to the 2. Fixtures and results tab and tap the match.

  1. Match tabs: Result, goals and cards (this screen), Scorekeeping and live, Referee report (it says pending until the referee sends it), Internal photos and Details and referees.

  2. Suspended players: the system shows in red which players CANNOT play this match and why (for example, a second yellow). There is no need to keep track by hand.

  3. Result: choose the Match status (Scheduled, Played, Forfeit, Suspended…), type each team's goals, the actual start and end times and, if you like, public remarks. Tap Save result.

  4. Goals and cards (official): what the scorekeeper enters on the phone appears here automatically. To add something manually: choose the Player, the Type (goal, yellow, red…), the Minute if you know it, the Quantity (for example 3 if they scored 3 goals) and a note. Tap Add.

  5. Scorekeeping sheet (to fill in): prints the blank sheet with the list of players, to fill in with a pen at the field.

  6. Completed scorekeeping sheet: prints the sheet with the data already recorded. There are also Blank sheet, Scorekeeping sheet (PDF), View on public page and Change history.

Scorekeeping and live tab

This is where you hand the match over to the scorekeeper who will fill it in from their phone (section 11).

  • Scorekeeper link: a special link for this match. The scorekeeper opens it on their phone and does not need a username or password. Tap Send via WhatsApp to send it to them, or Copy link. Open scorekeeping opens it on this same computer.

  • Change link (void the previous one): if the link reached the wrong person, tap it and the previous one stops working. Keep the link private.

  • Scorekeeping status: shows the status, times, how many players are present for each team and whether the scorekeeping is Open or closed. Close scorekeeping closes it from here.

  • Scorekeepers: you can register in advance who will be the scorekeeper (name, whether they are a board member or a delegate, and from which team). Tap Add scorekeeper.

  • Print / PDF scorekeeping sheet: the official match sheet.

Details and referees tab

  • Match details: change the date, time, field, phase (regular phase, quarterfinals, semifinal, final…), matchday, group or the home and away teams.

  • Referees officiating the match: choose how many referees officiate this match, who is the Center referee (they fill in the referee report) and the Assistant referee. If the referee is not on the list, type their name in Referee (free text).

  • Tap Save data. The red Delete match button deletes the match: use it with care.

The printed scorekeeping sheet

The sheet includes the date, time, field, matchday number, scorekeeper, observer, referee and the lists of both teams with number, ID card number and columns for attendance, goals (G), yellow card (YC) and red card (RC). Suspended players appear in red and crossed out. At the bottom are the captains' signatures.

To print or save as PDF tap Print / Save PDF and, under Printer or Destination, choose the printer or Save as PDF. On a phone: menu ⋮ → Share → Print → Save as PDF.

11. Scorekeeping from the phone, on match day

The scorekeeper fills in the match sheet on their phone, right at the field. Everything they record appears instantly on the public page ("Live"), in the standings and in the top scorers. If the internet goes down at the field, the scorekeeping keeps working: what is recorded is saved on the phone and sent automatically when the signal returns.

Step 1. Open the link and say who is filling in the scorekeeping

The scorekeeper opens the link they were sent via WhatsApp (section 10). Do this with internet access, before the match.

  1. At the top you see the teams, the score (0 - 0), the time and the match details: matchday, referees and rules (players on the field, substitutes, periods).

  2. In Your full name type your name.

  3. In You are choose whether you are a League board member or a team delegate.

  4. Tap Continue.

Step 2. The main scorekeeping screen

The picture shows the full phone screen split into three parts (read it from left to right, as if you were scrolling down with your finger).

  • Top: the score and the status (Not started, then the running time).

  • Verify player: to check a player's identity (step 4).

  • Match control: start time and the Start match button.

  • Players present: the list of players for each team, with boxes to check.

Step 3. Check the players present

  1. Tap Players present to open the list. In brackets you see how many each team has checked and the maximum allowed.

  2. Check the box for each player who is at the field, for both teams. Suspended players appear in red with the word SUSPENDED and cannot be checked. The system also does not let you check more than the maximum number of players.

  3. Tap Save players present at the end of the list.

Step 4. Verify identity and start

  1. Verify player: tap Scan ID card (QR) and point the camera at the QR code on the ID card: the player's photo and details appear so you can confirm it is them. If they have no ID card, type their ID card number, shirt number or name and tap Search.

  2. Start match: when the referee blows the whistle to start, tap this button and confirm. The clock starts running by itself. If you forgot to tap it on time, use Correct start / end times.

Step 5. Record goals and cards

  1. Player: choose the player from the list (only the players present appear).

  2. Minute: the system fills it in automatically from the clock; you can correct it. In Note you can write the reason (for example "red for assault").

  3. GOAL: tap to record the goal. There are also PENALTY (goal from a penalty) and OWN GOAL.

  4. YELLOW: yellow card. ASSIST records who gave the pass for the goal.

  5. RED: red card.

The score updates instantly and a green notice appears with what was recorded:

If you made a mistake: in Recorded there is a list of everything entered with its minute. Tap the ✕ next to the wrong entry to delete it and record it again correctly.

Step 6. Half-time and full time

  • When the first half ends, tap End of 1st half. When the second half begins, tap Start 2nd half.

  • When the referee blows the final whistle, tap End match and confirm.

Step 7. Close and send the scorekeeping

  1. View / print / PDF the scorekeeping sheet: review the complete sheet. You can save it as PDF or print it for the signatures.

  2. Close and send scorekeeping: tap it when everything is correct. The scorekeeping is closed and can no longer be changed from the phone. The standings, top scorers and suspensions update automatically.

If there are two scorekeepers (for example one from each team), use Is there another scorekeeper? Add them.

No internet at the field: if a notice appears saying there is no signal, keep recording as usual. The actions are saved on the phone with their time and sent automatically when the signal returns. Do not close the browser until the notice disappears.

12. Standings, discipline, bulletin and cumulative standings

There is nothing to calculate by hand. Every time a scorekeeping is closed or a result is saved, the system automatically updates the standings, top scorers, cards and suspended players.

Standings and top scorers

Open the championship and go to the 3. Standings and statistics tab.

  1. Official bulletin of the matchday: builds the official document for the matchday (see below). On the right are Season report (PDF), a complete summary of the championship, and Cumulative standings.

  2. Standings: ordered by points. The columns mean: P matches played, W won, D drawn, L lost, GF goals for, GA goals against, GD goal difference and PTS points.

Below is the Top scorers table, ordered by number of goals.

Cards and sanctions

Open the 4. Cards and sanctions tab.

  • Rules of this championship: reminds you how many yellow cards lead to a suspension and how many matches a red card carries. Change takes you to the settings.

  • Suspended for the next match: who cannot play, from which team, how many matches they still have to serve and why. These players appear blocked in the scorekeeping.

  • Cards per player: yellow and red cards for each player. The yellow at the limit label warns that one more yellow card will mean a suspension.

Further down on the same tab are the disciplinary committee's forms:

  1. Manual sanction for a player: choose the Player, the Sanction matches, the Fine in money (if any), From the date and the Reason (for example "assault on the referee"). Tap Record sanction.

  2. Team sanction: choose the Team, the Points to deduct and/or the Fine, the date and the reason (for example incidents involving the supporters or fielding an ineligible player). Tap Record sanction for the team. The points are deducted automatically from the standings and appear with an asterisk (*) on the public page.

  3. Fines pending payment: when the team pays a fine, tap Mark as paid.

Fines are charged automatically to the team's account (section 14).

Official bulletin of the matchday

The bulletin is the document the league publishes after each matchday. It is built automatically.

  • In Date choose the matchday.

  • The bulletin includes: 1) results and the player of the matchday, 2) standings and top scorers, 3) players suspended for the next matchday, 4) committee rulings (sanctions and appeals) and 5) the schedule for the next matchday with referees.

  • Tap Share via WhatsApp to send the link to the delegates, or Print / Save PDF to have it on paper or as a PDF.

Cumulative standings

They are used to add up two or more championships in an overall table (for example Apertura + Clausura) to decide the season final, relegations or qualifying places. Go to Championships → Σ Cumulative standings.

  1. In New cumulative table type the Name (for example "Cumulative table 2026").

  2. Check the Championships that count (2 or more).

  3. If you like, type a Note that appears below the table (for example "The top 2 play the season final").

  4. Leave Show it on the public page checked if you want people to see it.

  5. Tap Save. It appears in the list above; View shows it and Delete removes it.

13. Referees: registration, payment with invoice and referee portal

This is where you register the league's referees, pay them for the matches they officiated, and where each referee can send their report from their phone.

Register referees

Go to the Referees menu.

  1. Referee list: name, category, phone, whether they have access to the system, how many matches they officiated and their average rating (the rating is for internal use: the referee does not see it).

  2. New referee: type the Name (required), ID card number, phone and category. Tap Register referee.

  3. Email for their access and Password: these are optional. If you fill them in, the referee can log in from their phone to see their matches and fill in their reports. Give them the email and password.

Pay the referees

The system automatically adds up what is owed to each referee for the matches played, according to their role (center, assistant, fourth official). The amount per match is set in each championship: Settings → Money (section 7). Go to Finance → Referee payments.

  1. Pending payment: each referee with how many unpaid matches they have and how much they are owed.

  2. View and pay: tap the button of the referee you are going to pay.

33. Cycling with UCI rules: road, MTB, track, BMX and freestyle

The system scores cycling with UCI rules (International Cycling Union). Road and MTB are created as a racing championship; track, BMX Racing and BMX Freestyle as a multi-event championship (Championships → New championship).

Road: one-day races and stage races

  1. Create the race in 1. Races and times and set how many stages it has (1 = one-day race).

  2. Open 🚴 Stages and choose the type of each stage: Road stage, Individual time trial, Team time trial or Criterium.

  3. Finish bonus: seconds deducted from 1st, 2nd and 3rd (UCI: 10,6,4). Finish points for the points classification. Limit %: time limit (e.g. 12 = riders more than 12% behind the winner are out). Same time: riders less than 1 second behind the one ahead get the same time.

  4. Tap Save stages.

  5. Enter each rider's real time. In ⚑ enter intermediate sprint bonuses and points and mountain points.

The system builds automatically the general classification (sum of stages minus bonuses), points, mountains, best young rider (under-23: enter each rider's date of birth) and teams (the 3 best times of each club in each stage). In the team time trial the time is taken on the 3rd rider. Riders outside the time limit (OTL) or who do not finish a stage leave the GC.

MTB

Each race has a format: XCO (by laps; riders pulled by the 80% rule or lapped are ranked behind with “−N laps”), Downhill (best run) or Enduro (sum of stages).

Track

  • Kilometre, 500 m: by time. Scratch and elimination: enter the finishing place.

  • Sprint: enter the flying 200 m times and tap ⚔ Build the bracket: 1st vs 8th, 2nd vs 7th… best of 3 rides. Each next round is built automatically (semis, final and bronze).

  • Pursuit: qualifying by time; the best 2 race for gold and the 3rd and 4th for bronze.

  • Keirin: heats of up to 7 and a final of 6.

  • Points race and tempo: enter the place of the riders who score in each sprint (5-3-2-1, the finish double 10-6-4-2) and laps gained or lost (±20).

  • Omnium: enter the place in scratch, tempo and elimination (40, 38, 36… points) and the points won in the final points race.

BMX Racing

  1. Register the riders and tap 🏁 Build the motos (up to 8 per moto).

  2. In each ride enter the place or DNF, DNS, REL, DQ. Points are the place (lowest total wins).

  3. The best 4 of each moto qualify; the system builds the semifinals and the final of 8. With 8 riders or fewer, one moto of 3 rides decides.

BMX Freestyle (park and flatland)

Each judge scores 0 to 100 per run. In qualifying the average of the 2 runs counts; tap 🏆 Go to the final and in the final the best run counts.

  2. In each person's profile they choose the discipline and tap + Register. In duels they choose the category; in swimming and athletics they tick the events.

  3. They see each person's status (pending, approved or rejected with the reason), what is missing and their place in the table.

The system checks automatically: one ID = one delegation only, the event's age limits, the gender of each discipline and event, the maximum disciplines per person, the quota per delegation, schedule clashes (warns or blocks, depending on your settings) and the registration deadline.

4. Approving athletes

  1. In 👥 Athletes filter by delegation or status and tap the name to see the profile and documents.

  2. Tap ✓ Approve. If a required document is missing, the system warns you; you can use Approve without requirements. ✓ Approve all approves at once everyone who has everything complete.

  3. Once approved, the athlete goes into each championship automatically: in team sports, as a player of their delegation's team; in individual sports, as a competitor with their delegation as the club.

  4. ✕ Reject asks for the reason (the delegation sees it) and removes the athlete from the championships where they have not competed yet.

5. Points and medal table

  • Points per place: e.g. 10, 7, 5, 4, 3, 2, 1 or the Olympic 3, 2, 1. Each category or event of each discipline awards its places (multiplied by the discipline's factor).

  • The event is won by: whoever scores the most points or whoever has the most golds (Olympic medal table).

  • Points for taking part: for each discipline in which the delegation has approved athletes.

  • Scoring per delegation: in each category only the best N of each delegation score.

  • Count what is still being played: unticked, each category scores when it finishes.

  • In 🥇 Medal table and points → ➕ Extra points add points for the parade, supporters or fair play, or subtract them for sanctions (with the reason).

6. Registration rules and documents

In ⚙️ Settings choose whether delegations can register, until what date, whether you approve each athlete, whether documents are required to approve, the maximum disciplines per person and athletes per delegation, the minimum and maximum age and what to do with schedule clashes. Further down, in 📋 Details and documents of each athlete, choose what is requested (e.g. an employment certificate or company ID card to prove they are an employee).

7. Event administrators

  1. In ⚙️ Settings → 👤 Event administrators type name, email and password and tap Create and assign. Or assign the event to an existing user.

  2. That user (Event administrator profile) goes straight to their events: settings, disciplines and the results of their championships, delegations, athletes and medal table. They do not see the rest of the account (finances, other championships, users). You can have one or several.

  3. Assistants can also be given the 🏅 Olympics section (all events) in their permissions.

8. Public page

Each event has its page with the medal table, the results by discipline, the schedule and each delegation with its medals. Tap 📋 Copy link and share it. Your league's home page and each championship of the event link to the overall medal table. If you do not want it public, untick it in Settings (the delegations' links keep working).
