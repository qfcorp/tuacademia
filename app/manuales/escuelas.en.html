This manual explains, step by step and with pictures, how to run a sports school in TuAcademia: students, groups, attendance, monthly fee collection, coaches, training planning, each student's development, evaluation sessions, the club (youth divisions) and the family portal. It is up to date with version 17. You do not need to know about computers: just follow the red numbers on each picture.

📅 Not using TuAcademia yet? Request your demo (7 days with all modules) at tuacademia.tukanec.com/solicitar-demo or write to us on WhatsApp. We will set up your access and help you load your league or school.

1. How to use this manual and who uses what

TuAcademia is an online system: nothing needs to be installed. It opens in the browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari) on your computer or phone, at your school's web address.

How to read the pictures: each screenshot has red circles with numbers (1, 2, 3…) and a red border around the important part. Below the picture you will find what each number means and what you should do there.

Words used throughout the manual:

  • Tap or click: press with your finger (phone) or with the left mouse button (computer).

  • Menu: the list of options at the top (on a phone it opens with the ☰ button).

  • Save: the green button at the end of each form. If you do not tap it, your changes are lost.

  • Group or category: a set of students who train together with a schedule and a coach (for example U-10 Monday and Wednesday).

  • Charge: something a student has to pay (monthly fee, registration fee, uniform…).

  • Parent/guardian: the father, mother or person responsible for the student.

2. Getting the school started

The first time, follow these 8 steps in order. After that, the daily work is just taking attendance and collecting payments.

  1. Settings (section 5): school details, colors, monthly fee and registration fee amounts, due day, sibling discount and venues.

  2. Coaches (section 11): register them with their payment method and give them their access.

  3. Groups and schedules (section 6): age categories (U-8, U-10…) or open ones, with days, times, field, venue and coach.

  4. Students (section 7): the full record with their parent/guardian, or let parents register them online (section 18).

  5. Attendance (section 8): the coach takes the roll in every class from their phone.

  6. Monthly fees (section 10): they are generated automatically each month with their due date.

  7. Collections and receipts (section 10): in cash, by bank transfer with proof of payment, or by card; the receipt is issued automatically.

  8. Evaluations (section 12): evaluation sessions, measurements, tests with a traffic light, technical evaluation and the development report for parents.

Then, every week, the coach plans their classes in Training sessions (section 9) and, when it is time, the school holds an evaluation session (section 12). If you are a club whose categories play matches, enable youth divisions in Settings (section 5).

3. Logging in and My account

To log in you need the email and password given to you by QF Producciones (or by the school owner). It is the same screen for the owner, the office staff and the coaches. Parents do not log in here: they have their own portal (section 20).

  1. Email: type your full email, for example admin@loscracks.ec.

  2. Password: type your password. Upper and lower case letters matter.

  3. Log in: tap the green button. You will go to the Overview screen (section 4).

  4. Forgot your password?: tap this link, type your email and you will receive a message to create a new one.

  5. The family portal: this link takes you to the parents' login. Send it to the families.

Tip: on your phone, open the system's address and choose "Add to Home screen" or "Install". An icon will appear like any other app.

My account and security

In the top menu, tap My account.

  • Change my password: type your current password, then the new one twice (at least 8 characters) and tap Change password.

  • Two-step verification (recommended for the owner): tap Enable two-step verification. Install a code app on your phone (Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator), scan the code that appears and type the 6 digits. From then on, in addition to your password, you will be asked for those 6 digits when you log in; they change every 30 seconds.

  • Log out: when you use someone else's computer, always tap Log out when you are done.

4. The Overview screen

This is the first screen after logging in. It shows at a glance how the school is doing today: who is training, how much was collected and who owes money. To come back here from anywhere, tap Overview in the menu.

  1. Main menu: Overview, Students, Groups and schedules, Attendance, Training sessions, Evaluations, Club (if enabled), Payments, Coaches, Shop, Activity log, Settings, Users, My plan and My account. Each option has its own section in this manual.

  2. School bar: the school's name, the sport, the view public page link and your profile.

  3. Figures: active students, attendance percentage over the last 30 days, the amount collected this month and the amount still to be collected.

  4. Training today: the groups that have class today, with schedule, venue, number of students and coach. The Take attendance button opens that group's list (section 8).

  5. Overdue payments: the students who owe money, how much and since when. Tap the name to open their record. The phone button (📲) opens WhatsApp with a payment reminder ready to send to the parent/guardian.

Further down is the list of Injured students, so coaches know who should not train.

Since version 17, the Overview also shows training sessions:

  1. Training sessions: the menu for planning classes (section 9).

  2. Club: appears if the school has enabled youth divisions (section 13).

  3. Classes planned for the week: how many of the classes in the next 7 days already have a plan. Tap it to go to the calendar.

  4. Today's plan: each group training today shows the class objective, or the link + Plan today's class.

5. School settings

This is where you enter the school's fixed details and the amounts you charge. You fill them in only once when you start. Go to the Settings menu. The screen has 4 parts; each one has its own Save button.

School details

  • Name, City or area and Sport.

  • Logo: tap Choose File ("Elegir archivo" in Spanish browsers) and select the image.

  • Primary and secondary color: tap the color box and choose. They are used on the public page, ID cards and receipts.

  • Contact, Phone or WhatsApp and Email of the school.

  • Payment details: bank, account type and number, and the account holder's name. Families see them in their portal and on receipts.

Charges

  • General monthly fee ($): the standard monthly fee. Each group or student can have their own amount (sections 6 and 7).

  • Registration fee ($): charged once, when a new student is approved.

  • Monthly fee due day: for example 5 = due on the 5th of each month.

  • Sibling discount (%): applied only from the second child of the same parent/guardian (same ID card number).

Online registration

  • Check Online registration open so parents can fill in the form from the school's page (section 18).

  • In Text parents see, write requirements, schedules and prices.

  • Below are the Link to share and the WhatsApp button to send it.

Venues

If the school trains in several places, create one venue for each: type the Name and the Address under New venue and tap + Add venue. If a venue is no longer used, uncheck Active and tap Save.

Youth divisions (club mode)

This is for clubs and academies whose categories play matches (U-10 to U-19). It adds the Club menu and the Club tab in each player's record: matches and minutes, maturation, coaching staff notes, assessments, school and infirmary (section 13). If your school is recreational, leave it off and the menu stays simpler.

  1. Check Enable youth divisions in this school.

  2. Tap Save. If you turn it off later, the data is kept.

If you see “Your current plan does not include this section”, you need the Complete School plan.

6. Groups and schedules

A group is a set of students who train together: it has its category, its coach, its days and its schedule. The category can be U-N (by birth year: in 2026, a child born in 2016 is U-10) or open (all ages). Go to the Groups and schedules menu.

Each card is a group and shows:

  • The name and the category (for example "U-7 morning — born 2019–2020").

  • The days and times, the field, the venue, the coach and how many students it has out of its capacity (for example 7 of 20).

  • Buttons: View students (the group's list), Attendance (take the roll, section 8), ID cards (print the group's ID cards, section 15) and Edit.

  • Play in a TuAcademia league (optional): if a league that uses TuAcademia has a championship with open registration, you can register this group as a team: choose the Championship, type the Team name and the Delegate's details, and tap Send request to the league. When the league approves it, the group's active students are added as players with their photos and authorizations. It is not mandatory.

Create or edit a group

To create a group, scroll down to the end of the screen, to the + New group form. To change an existing one, tap its Edit button.

  1. Name: for example "U-10 afternoon".

  2. Category: choose U-age (by birth year) or Open category. If it is U-age, type the range of years in which the students were born (a single year = a single category).

  3. Venue and Coach: choose from the lists (venues are created in Settings and coaches in Coaches).

  4. Training days: check the days (for example Mon, Wed, Fri).

  5. From and Up to: the start and end time. Field: the name of the field.

  6. Maximum capacity: how many students fit (0 = no limit).

  7. This group's monthly fee: leave it empty to use the general monthly fee from Settings, or type another amount if this group pays differently.

  8. Leave Active checked and tap Save group.

7. Students: registration and each student's record

Each student has a record with everything: details, parent/guardian, health, payments, attendance, measurements, tests, technical evaluation, development, club and report. Go to the Students menu.

  1. + New student: opens the registration form (see below).

  2. ID cards: prints the ID cards (section 15).

  3. Filters: choose a group or type the name, ID number or parent/guardian and tap Search. At the top are the tabs Active, Pending approval (those who registered online, section 18) and Withdrawn.

  4. List: each student with their category, group, parent/guardian and phone, attendance percentage and payment status: Up to date (green) or Expired (red, with the amount owed). Tap the name to open their record.

Register a new student

  1. Student: First names (required), Last names, ID number and Date of birth (required: it sets the U-age category). Then Gender, Position, Dominant foot, School and Address.

  2. Group: choose the group. If you leave it empty, the system assigns it automatically by age.

  3. Photo: tap Choose File (on a phone you can take it right then). Check Their photo can be published only if the parent/guardian authorizes it.

  4. Parent/guardian: Name, ID number, Relationship (mother, father…), Mobile with WhatsApp and Email. Important: siblings are recognized by the same parent/guardian ID number, and that is how the sibling discount is applied automatically.

  5. Medical record and emergency (tap to open): blood type, allergies, illnesses, medications, health insurance and emergency contact.

  6. Student payments (tap to open): Special monthly fee, Scholarship (%), Due day and Start date, if this student pays differently from the others.

  7. Leave Charge registration fee checked if it applies, check I have the guardian's authorization (Ecuador's Personal Data Protection Law) and tap Register student.

The student's record

Tap a student's name to open their record. At the top you see their photo, category, age, group, position, attendance over the last 90 days and whether they are up to date with payments.

  1. ID card and Report: shortcuts to view or print the ID card (section 15) and the term's development report.

  2. Record tabs: Data, Development, Club (if enabled), Health and injuries, Payments, Attendance and log, Measurements, Tests, Technique and Report. They are explained one by one below.

  3. Family portal: the parent/guardian sees on their phone what their children trained each day, attendance, payments, development charts, the report and the ID card, and can upload the proof of payment.

  4. Generate access for the guardian: tap this button to create the family's access. A message appears, ready to send via WhatsApp with the instructions (section 20).

Further down in Data is the same registration form to correct any detail (tap Save at the end). Below it there are two buttons: Remove from school (stops generating monthly fees; the student moves to the Withdrawn tab and can be Reactivated) and Void QR ID card (if the card was lost).

Development tab

It shows how the student is progressing, with charts. It is the same thing their parents see in the portal (without the internal notes). At the top is the Report to print or PDF button (see Report tab below).

  1. Development tab of the record.

  2. Figures: attendance over the last 3 months, classes and hours trained in the year, how many tests they improved in and how many badges they have.

  3. Badges: they are earned automatically. Perfect attendance (did not miss any class last month), Consistency (90% or more), Personal best, School record, Group's biggest improvement, Technique on the rise, Goal achieved and Top scorer (in the club).

  4. Attendance by month: green from 85%, yellow from 65%, red below that.

  5. Minutes trained per week, with the average line.

  1. Category radar: the further out, the better compared with the other students in the same category (U-N) at the school. The dotted yellow line is the average.

  2. Test table: first and last result, how much they improved (green ▲) or dropped (red ▼), and the traffic light: green = very good, yellow = average, red = needs improvement for their age.

  3. One chart per test: their progress (blue) against their category average (dotted yellow).

  1. Technical radar: the latest evaluation (blue), the previous one (dotted gray) and their group's average (yellow).

  2. Stars for each skill with ▲ or ▼ if it changed, and the coach's comment. Below are the height, weight and BMI charts.

Personal goals

At the end of the tab you set concrete goals for the student. When they achieve one, they earn a badge.

  1. Each goal with its area, date and progress bar.

  2. Refresh: move the progress, change the status (In progress, Achieved or On hold), write a comment and tap Save. When it reaches 100% it becomes Achieved. Delete removes it.

  3. New goal: write it (for example “Directional first touch with the weaker foot”), choose the area and the date. Leave Parents can see it checked if you want them to see it.

  4. + Add goal.

Club tab

It appears only if the school has enabled youth divisions (section 5). It brings together everything about the player in the club.

  1. Season figures: matches played, as a starter, minutes, goals and assists, and average rating.

  2. Minutes in recent matches: blue if they started, gray if they came off the bench. Next to it, the list of recent matches.

  3. Maturation and growth: how long until (or how long since) their growth spurt, at what age it will happen and a training tip. Also how many cm they grow per year and the estimated adult height. To calculate it you need the sitting height in Measurements and the father's and mother's height (entered here).

  4. Bar: before the growth spurt (gray), at the peak (yellow) or after (green).

  5. Coaching staff assessment: potential A, B or C, position, profile, strengths, areas to improve and the recommendation. + New assessment adds another one.

  6. Academic performance: average grade, attendance and behavior for each school period. + Record period adds one.

  7. Coaching staff notes: each area (coach, fitness, psychology, nutrition, medical, tutor, management) writes its notes. Confidential notes are seen only by management and that same area. The family never sees these notes.

Payments tab

  1. Pending: what the student owes, with its due date. Overdue items say expired in red. If the family sent a proof of payment, the proof of payment link appears so you can see it.

  2. History: all charges with their status (Pending, Expired or Paid with the date). Receipt reprints the receipt for a payment; Void deletes a wrong charge.

  3. Charge and issue receipt: check what is going to be paid, choose the payment method (cash, bank transfer…) and tap the button. The receipt comes out ready to print or send (section 10).

  4. Other charge: to charge something that is not the monthly fee (registration fee, uniform, tournament…). Choose the Type, type the Description, the Value and the date it Expires, and tap Add.

Attendance and log tab

At the top it shows how many training sessions they attended in the last 90 days and each day with its letter (Present, Late, Excused or Absent). Attendance is taken in section 8.

  1. Upcoming planned classes of their group.

  2. Training log: each class with its objective, minutes, intensity, coach and the exercises they did.

  3. Coach's note for this student that day.

  4. Class photos. Parents see this same log in their portal.

Health and injuries tab

  1. Medical record: blood type, allergies, conditions, medications, insurance and emergency contact (with their phone, which you can tap to call). It is edited in the Data tab → Medical record.

  2. Record injury: type the date, the area (for example right ankle), the type (for example sprain), the approximate days out and the treatment. Tap Save. The student appears under Injured in the Overview until you tap Discharge.

Each injury is followed in phases until discharge: rest, treatment, rehabilitation, return to the group and discharge. When you record the injury you can also enter the diagnosis and the professional treating it; the return date is calculated from the days out.

  1. Phase bar: green for what is completed, yellow for the current phase.

  2. Estimated return: the date and how many days are left (it warns you if it has already passed).

  3. + Log progress: choose the date, the new phase, change the estimated return if needed, write the details (“already jogging without pain”) and tap Save. When you choose the Discharge phase, the student is discharged.

Measurements tab

  1. Measurement history: weight, height, waist, hip, arm, thigh, body fat percentage, arm span and BMI (calculated automatically). At the top, charts of how weight and height are evolving.

  2. New measurement: type the date and the measurements taken (you do not need to fill in all of them) and tap Save.

If the school has enabled the club (section 5), the measurement also asks for the sitting height: seated on a bench with a straight back, measure from the bench to the top of the head. It is used to calculate maturation (Club tab).

Tests tab

Each card is a physical test (30-meter sprint, jumps, sit-ups, flexibility, endurance, dribbling, ball control…) with a chart of how they have improved and their best mark.

  1. Record result: choose the Test, the date, type the Result and tap Save. To record a whole group at once, use Evaluations → Tests (section 12).

Technique tab

  1. Spider chart: shows the latest technical evaluation (solid line) compared with the previous one (dotted line), with the coach's comment.

  2. New technical evaluation: rate from 1 (needs improvement) to 5 (excellent).

  3. Each skill: ball control, passing, dribbling, taking on players, shooting, heading, defending, game vision and attitude. Tap the number; the chosen one turns blue. Write a comment if you wish.

  4. Save evaluation.

Report tab

The development report brings together the term: attendance, hours trained, badges, test charts compared with their category, the technical evaluation, what types of exercises they trained, measurements, goals and the coach's comment. It replaces the old report card. The family also sees it in their portal and can save it as a PDF.

  1. Term: choose the period (for example July – September 2026).

  2. View / print the report: opens the report ready to print or save as PDF.

  3. Coach's comment: write the comment for the term and tap Save comment.

8. Attendance: taking the roll at every training session

The coach takes the roll in every class, usually from their phone on the field. It takes less than a minute. Attendance is added automatically to each student's record, the development report and the family portal. Go to the Attendance menu, or tap Take attendance in the Overview (section 4).

Step by step:

  1. At the top: choose the group and the date (today's date is preselected).

  2. All present: tap this button to mark everyone as present at once. Then correct only those who were absent.

  3. Next to each student tap a letter: P present (green), T arrived late, J excused absence, A absent. A student with 🩹 next to them is injured.

  4. Class coach: check who taught the class and leave The coach taught the class checked. This counts toward the coach's pay (section 11).

  5. What was trained: the Objective and the exercises of the class. If the class was planned in Training sessions (section 9), they are already loaded; just correct what changed. See below for notes, effort and photos.

  6. Tap Save attendance.

Below is the Month's history: a table with each student and each class day, with their letter and the attendance percentage for the month. Tap a day to open and correct that list.

Note for each student

In addition to the letter, the coach can leave a short note for each student. The family sees it in their child's training log.

  1. Class status: Planned (it has a plan, attendance is missing), Held (attendance already taken) or Canceled. When you save the attendance it changes to Held.

  2. Note button: tap it next to the student. It turns green when the student already has a note.

  3. The note: choose Very good, To improve or Note, and write the message, for example “Excellent control with the left foot”. If you check Only for the school, the family does not see it.

Exercises, effort and class photos

  1. Exercises: those of the class. You can change, remove or add them.

  2. Duration: how many minutes the class lasted. They add up to each student's hours trained.

  3. How hard was the class?: tap a number from 1 (very easy) to 10 (maximum). It helps you manage the group's training load.

  4. Photos already uploaded for this class.

  5. Upload photos: you can choose several at once from your phone. Leave Parents can see the photos checked if you want them to see them.

  6. Show this class to parents: if you uncheck it, the class does not appear in the families' log.

  7. Save attendance.

Photos are private: they are seen only by the school and by the families of the students who were in that class. To delete a photo, use Delete photos below the Save button.

9. Training sessions: planning each class

The coach prepares their classes in advance with exercises from the school's library. When they take attendance, that class is marked as held and parents see on their phone what their child trained. Go to the Training sessions menu.

  1. Tabs: Planning (the calendar), Exercise library and Work cycles.

  2. Group: choose the group you are going to plan. Coaches only see their own groups.

  3. Month: use the arrows to move to the previous or next month.

  4. Month's figures: classes according to the group's schedule, how many have been held, how many are planned and how many do not have a plan yet (in red).

  5. Cycle: the work theme for those weeks (see Cycles below).

  6. Class held (green): attendance has already been taken. It shows how many students came.

  7. Class planned (yellow): it already has a plan but has not been held yet. It shows how many exercises and minutes it has. Training days without a plan show + Plan. A canceled class appears in red and crossed out.

Below the calendar there are two charts: how the month's time was used (technical, tactical, physical, games…) and how many students came to each class.

Planning a class

Tap a day on the calendar (a planned class or + Plan).

  1. Class objective: what you want to achieve, for example “Short passing and directional first touch”. It is the first thing parents see.

  2. Cycle: it is chosen automatically according to the date; you can change it.

  3. Duration and intensity: the class minutes and how hard it will be, from 1 to 10.

  4. Exercises: in each row choose an exercise from the library (or “other” and type it), the part of the class (warm-up, main or cool-down), the minutes and a variation if you wish. The X removes the row.

  5. + Add exercise: adds another row. Next to it you see the total minutes.

  6. Repeat every week until: if you choose a date, the same plan is copied to that day of every week (without touching classes already held).

  7. Save plan. The Parents see the plan checkbox decides whether families see this class in their portal.

  8. Copy this plan: check other groups (and another date if you wish) and tap Copy.

  9. Cancel the class: type the reason (rain, holiday, field taken) and tap Suspend. A canceled class does not count toward attendance or the coach's pay. It can be restored with Reactivate. Delete plan removes the plan for that day.

When the day comes, the Take attendance button opens the group's list with the plan already loaded (section 8).

Exercise library

The school starts with 17 sample exercises and can add its own.

  1. Type: shows only one type (warm-up, technical, tactical, physical, games, goalkeepers or cool-down). Check View archived to see the ones you no longer use.

  2. + New exercise: opens the form below.

  3. Exercise card: minutes, players, ages, how many times it was used, objective, how it is done and equipment. If it has a video, the Video button appears.

  4. Edit or Archive (it is hidden from the list but not deleted from the classes where it was already used).

Fill in the Name, Type, Minutes, Objective and How it is done. If you wish, add players, equipment, ages, a YouTube link (it must start with https://) and a diagram or photo of the exercise. Tap Save exercise.

Work cycles

A cycle is a block of weeks with a theme, for example “Passing and control” for 4 weeks. It helps show parents that the work follows a plan.

  1. + New cycle: type the name, the group (or the whole school), from and to dates, the objective and the content for each week. Tap Save cycle.

  2. In progress / Next / Finished: the status is set automatically according to the dates. The cycle in progress has a green border.

  3. Progress bar: how much of the cycle has passed and how many classes it has.

10. Payments: monthly fees, collections, receipts and cards

Monthly fees are generated automatically every month for each active student, with their amount (general, group or special), their sibling discounts and scholarships, and their due date. You just collect the payment and the receipt is issued automatically. Go to the Payments menu.

Payment status

  1. Tabs: Payment status (this screen), Receipts of payment (bank transfers sent by families), + Charges to a group and Cash box for the month. At the top right is Card payments.

  2. Traffic light: how many students are Up to date (green), Expiring soon in the next 5 days (yellow) and Expired (red). Tap a number to see only those students.

The list shows each student with their group, status, the amount owed and the next due date. You can filter by group.

  • Charge: opens the student's Payments tab (section 7) to record the payment.

  • Phone button (📲): appears for those who owe; it opens WhatsApp with a payment reminder ready to send to the parent/guardian.

How to collect a payment and give the receipt

  1. In the list, tap Charge next to the student (or open their record, Payments tab).

  2. Under Pending, check what is going to be paid.

  3. Choose the payment method: Cash, Transfer, etc.

  4. Tap Charge and issue receipt.

  5. The numbered receipt comes out, ready to print, save as PDF or send:

To print or save as PDF, tap Print / Save PDF and choose the printer or Save as PDF. On a phone: menu ⋮ → Share → Print → Save as PDF.

Approving proofs of payment sent by families

When a family pays by bank transfer, they upload a photo of the proof of payment from their phone (section 20). The red number on the Payments menu tells you there are proofs of payment to review.

  1. Photo of the proof of payment: tap it to see it full size. Next to it are the student, the amount, what is being paid and who sent it. First check with your bank that the money arrived.

  2. Approve and issue receipt: if the money arrived, tap this button. The payment and the receipt are recorded automatically and the family sees it in their portal.

  3. Reject: if the money did not arrive or the photo cannot be read, tap Reject. The family sees that it was rejected.

Charges to a group or to everyone

To charge something other than the monthly fee to several students at once (uniform, tournament entry, trip, insurance).

  1. Under To whom, choose a group or All active students.

  2. Choose the Type, type the Description (for example "Summer Cup Tournament") and the Amount per student.

  3. Choose the date it Expires and tap Add charge. It is added to each student's account.

Cash box for the month

It shows how much money came in and went out during the month.

  • Choose the month at the top. You will see Income, Expenses and the Result (income minus expenses).

  • Income is broken down by item (monthly fees, shop…) and so are Expenses (coach payments, other costs…).

  • Record expense: to log what the school pays (field rental, balls, cones…). Choose the Description category, type the Description, the Value, the Date and the payment Form, and tap Save expense. Payments to coaches are recorded automatically (section 11).

Card payments

Families can pay by credit or debit card from their phone. TuAcademia receives the money through Plux, applies the payment, issues the receipt instantly and transfers the amount collected to the school's account. Go to Payments → Card payments.

  1. Check Receive card payments from my families.

  2. Under Who pays the card fee? choose The school covers it (the family pays the exact amount and the school receives the amount minus the fee) or Added to the amount the payer pays (for example, they owe $20.00 and pay $21.19; the school receives the full $20.00).

  3. Type the Account where we transfer what is collected (bank, account type and number, account holder, ID card number or RUC (Ecuador's taxpayer number)) and tap Save.

  4. In Payment report, choose the dates and tap View, or download the report in Excel.

At the top you see the amount collected by card, the fees, the net amount for the school and what is still to be transferred. The current costs are shown on the same screen.

11. Coaches and payslip with invoice

This is where you register coaches, see how much each one is owed for the month and print their payslip. If the coach provides an invoice, it is uploaded together with the payment. Go to the Coaches menu.

  • Choose the Month at the top.

  • Each coach shows: their groups and students, how they are paid (per class taught, fixed monthly salary or per active student), the classes taught in the month (with their absences) and what they are owed.

  • View: opens the coach's details to pay them.

Registering a new coach

  4. Check the personal data authorization box and tap Save. Only the correct rows are saved.

For students: if you leave the Group column empty, the system places them in the group that matches their birth year. If they are under 18, the parent/guardian (name, ID number and phone) is required. Monthly fees are generated automatically.

Downloading all my data

An Excel file with one sheet per topic (venues, coaches, groups, students, payments and attendance). It contains personal data: keep it in a safe place. Every download is recorded in the activity log.

25. Student videos

Upload a short video (2 minutes maximum) for a student: their technique, their improvement, a moment from training. It reaches their portal so they can watch and download it. It is deleted automatically after 10 days.

  1. Choose the student.

  2. Type a title (and a message if you wish).

  3. Choose the video from your phone (MP4 or MOV). If it is longer than 2 minutes, the system warns you before uploading it.

  4. Tap Upload video and wait for the bar to reach 100%.

There is also a 🎬 Upload video button in each student's record. In the list of videos you have 💬 Notify to send the notice to the parents via WhatsApp.

In the parents' portal, 🎬 Has a new video appears, with the ⬇ Download button and the date on which it will be deleted.

Videos can be stored on the server or on a separate drive (the laptop): the system administrator sets this up in Settings → Videos.

26. Indicators, Refer and earn, language and federation

📈 Indicators

In Settings → Indicators you see how your school is doing month by month: active students, income, monthly fees collected, overdue debt, attendance, occupancy of each group, and new and withdrawn students.

🤝 Refer and earn

In Settings → Refer and earn you have your personal link. Share it with other leagues, schools or federations: when they sign up, you earn discount months on your plan. On the same screen you see who you referred and how many months you have earned.

🌐 Languages

With the 🌐 button in the top bar, each person can view the system in Spanish, English, French, Portuguese or Italian. In Settings → Public page language you choose the language in which your public page opens.

This manual is also available in all five languages: at tuacademia.tukanec.com/manual tap the 🌐 button and choose the language. The PDF is downloaded in the chosen language.

🏛 Joining a federation

If your school belongs to a federation that also uses TuAcademia, type in Settings → Federation the code the federation gave you (it starts with FED-) and tap Request affiliation. Once the federation approves it, your tournaments remain yours alone, but the federation sees your figures and can apply federation sanctions.

27. Taekwondo, karate and judo: belts, poomsae and belt exams

If your school teaches taekwondo, karate or judo, the system includes everything a dojang or dojo needs: your belt list, the poomsae or kata for each belt, belt exams with fees, and certificates with a QR code. It's under Students → 🥋 Belt exams.

To make it appear, create a group in Groups and schedules and in the Sport field choose Taekwondo, Karate or Judo. If your whole school is taekwondo, also choose it as the main sport in Settings. The wording changes automatically: in taekwondo it says poomsae, kyorugi, sabonim and dojang; in karate and judo it says kata, kumite, sensei and dojo.

⚙ Step 1: review your belt list

The first time, the system loads the default list. In taekwondo it is the kup/dan system with tips: 10th Kup White, 9th Kup White with yellow tip, 8th Kup Yellow… up to 1st Kup Red with black tip and then the black Dan belts, each with its poomsae (Taegeuk Il Jang, Ee Jang… Koryo, Keumgang). In karate it includes the kyu grades with the Heian, Tekki, Bassai and Kanku katas; in judo, the kyu and dan grades.

  1. Go to the ⚙ Belts and poomsaes tab.

  2. Change whatever your school does differently: the rank, name, color, tip (the second color) and poomsae of each belt. Uncheck Active on the ones you don't use.

  3. To add a belt, use the empty rows at the end. The Order number determines each student's "next belt".

  4. Tap 💾 Save belts. If you make a mistake, the ↺ Restore the default list button puts it back the way it was (students' grades already recorded are not deleted).

📖 Step 2: each student's poomsae

In the 📖 Students' poomsaes tab you see each student with their belt, the next one and the poomsae they must perform to move up. Tap 📖 Learning when they start it and ✅ Mastered when they know it. Parents see it in their portal, so they know what to practice at home.

📝 Step 3: create the belt exam

  1. In the 📝 Exams tab, fill in + New belt exam: date, time, place, examiner (e.g.: Sabonim Carlos Andrade, 4th Dan) and the exam fee in dollars (leave it at 0 if you don't charge).

  2. Tap Create exam. The exam opens so you can register students.

✅ Step 4: register, charge and enter results

  1. Open + Register students. Each student already comes with their next belt selected (you can change it if they skip a grade). Check the ones taking the exam and tap Register selected.

  2. Tap 💵 Charge exam fee: a charge is created for each registered student, which appears in Payments and in the parent portal so they can pay it like a monthly fee. It isn't duplicated if you tap it again.

  3. On exam day, print the 🖨 Exam record (the list with space for signatures).

  4. After the exam, give each student their score, mark whether they master the poomsae, choose the result (✅ Passed, ✗ Did not pass or — Did not attend) and tap 💾 Save results.

  5. Tap 🥋 Close exam and award belts. Students who passed get their new belt recorded: it shows on their profile, on their ID card and in the parent portal.

If you made a mistake in a result, tap Reopen to correct, change it and close it again: the system removes or adds the belt automatically.

🏅 Step 5: certificates with QR code

Once the exam is closed, the 🏅 Certificates for those who passed button appears: one landscape page per student, with the school logo, their name, the belt in its colors, the date, the examiner and a QR code. Tap Print / Save PDF. You can also print a single student's certificate with the 🏅 Certificate button on their row.

Anyone who scans the QR sees ✓ VALID CERTIFICATE on their phone with the school's real data. If the certificate was altered or voided, it says ✗ INVALID. It can also be verified by typing the code at tuacademia.tukanec.com/certificado.php.

🥊 Fights and weight categories

In Students → Competitions, record your students' fights (kyorugi or kumite). The weight categories list already includes the official ones: World Taekwondo by age (children, cadet 12-14, junior 15-17 and adult), WKF for karate and IJF for judo. When you choose the student, the system suggests their category based on their last recorded weight. In the 🥋 Belts tab you can record a grade the student already had or earned at another school.

28. Sport modules: your school's sports

Your school has one or more sport modules. In Groups and schedules you can only choose sports from your modules. To add one, request a quote in ⭐ My plan → Sports in your account: the plan includes one sport and each additional sport is quoted on request. Each sport asks for its own data and documents in the student record; see 📋 Requirements and documents.

29. New sports in the school

Since version 36, in Groups and schedules you can also create 🎾 squash, ♟️ chess, 🎮 e-sports, 🏋️ weightlifting, 🏊 triathlon, ⚾ baseball, 🥎 softball and 🤾 handball groups (if your account has that module).

  1. When creating the group, choose the sport.

  2. The system loads that sport's built-in tests, skills and exercises (for example, in squash: serves into the box, straight drives and ghosting; in chess: mate problems, tactics and endgames).

  3. In squash, chess and e-sports, students record their matches or games in the club, with their win-loss record.

If your school also runs tournaments (squash in groups, Swiss-system chess, road races…), those championships are created just like in a league: see the league manual.
